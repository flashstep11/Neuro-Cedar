How This Study Works

Welcome to the {{ platform_name }} study. Please read the following instructions carefully. You will be tested on this information before proceeding.

The Investment Task

You will participate in a simulated investment on a fictional platform called {{ platform_name }}.

  • You begin with a virtual account balance of Rs 1,000.
  • A fixed Rs {{ initial_invest }} is committed to the platform automatically — this is your initial investment.
  • The platform will show you a return on your initial investment.
  • You can then choose to reinvest an additional amount, from Rs 0 to a maximum of Rs {{ max_reinvest }} (in steps of Rs 50). This is the maximum reinvestment.
  • After reinvesting, the platform applies a randomly determined return to your reinvested amount. The platform value can rise or fall — roughly {{ pool_min_pct }}% to +{{ pool_max_pct }}% from your reinvestment.
  • You will then be given the option to withdraw your funds (which involves a processing fee that will be explained at that stage).

Lottery Prize

At the end of the study, {{ prize_count }} unique Rs {{ prize_value }} gift vouchers will be awarded by lottery to participants.

  • Your number of lottery entries equals your final balance ÷ {{ lottery_div }}.
  • A higher final balance means more entries and a better chance of winning a voucher.
  • No real deposit is required — all balances in this study are virtual.

Example: if your final balance is Rs 950, you have 95 lottery entries.

Summary — Key Facts

(You will be quizzed on these.)

  1. Your initial investment on the platform is Rs {{ initial_invest }}.
  2. The maximum reinvestment you can choose is Rs {{ max_reinvest }}.
  3. The platform value can rise or fall (roughly {{ pool_min_pct }}% to +{{ pool_max_pct }}%).
  4. Your lottery entries are determined by your final balance.
