What Was This Study About?

This study examined how app-based investment fraud tactics influence decision-making. Specifically, we were interested in how features of an investment interface affect people's choices about reinvesting money and paying withdrawal fees.

What Was Simulated

The following elements were part of the study design:

  • Return on initial investment — Some participants saw a 0% return and some saw a 35% return on the initial Rs 100. This was randomly determined and was intended to study how initial returns affect subsequent decisions. The apparent gain was simulated.
  • Social proof cues — Some participants saw a live investor count and pop-ups of fictional investor activity (e.g., "Aarav K. from Pune just reinvested"). These were entirely made up and not based on any real data.
  • Urgency timer — Some participants saw a countdown labelled "Gold-tier reinvestment window closes in…". It was non-binding: at zero it only showed "Window extended" and had no effect on the study or outcomes.
  • Status and pressure cues — Some participants saw a "Silver Member, 72% to Gold" card (fixed at 72% for everyone), an "Only 3 Gold slots left" notice, member reviews and messages from an "account manager". All of these were made up and did not respond to any choice.
  • Withdrawal charges — Everyone who paid the Rs {{ f1 }} processing fee was then asked for a Rs {{ f2 }} verification fee, and only paying both released the balance. This "one more fee" sequence is a common tactic in real app-based investment fraud. {% if freeze_enabled %}Some participants also saw an "account freeze" countdown; it was non-binding and had no effect.{% endif %} The Rs {{ cap }} reinvestment limit was set so that everyone always had enough cash to pay both charges. Once both charges are counted, reinvesting rarely paid off, which mirrors how real fraud apps work. All balances were virtual and no real money was involved.

About the Pool Return

The return applied to each reinvestment (which could be roughly {{ pool_min_pct }}% to +{{ pool_max_pct }}%) was randomly determined and was independent of:

  • Your reward condition (0% or 35% initial return)
  • Your earlier pool result (each round was drawn separately)
  • The interface you saw (neutral or persuasive)
  • The amount you chose to reinvest

Lottery

The lottery is real. Four Rs 250 gift vouchers will be awarded to participants by draw, weighted by final balance. Winners will be contacted using the details they provide.

Red Flags to Remember

  • Never pay a fee, "tax" or deposit to release a withdrawal. Real platforms deduct charges; they do not ask you to pay first.
  • Countdowns, "limited slots" and "live" investor counts are designed to stop you from thinking it through.
  • Early profits shown inside an app are just numbers until the money reaches your bank account.
  • Badges and certificates inside an app prove nothing. Check registration on the regulator's own website.
